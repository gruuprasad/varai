# Varai Product Context

## Register

Product application.

## Users

People building software through AI builders, especially people who do not
read or want to manage implementation code. They still need to understand,
direct, and remain accountable for the system being built.

## Purpose

Varai is the human-level interface to application development. It preserves a
human-ratified model of the application, gives builders that intent, and shows
whether independently observed implementation evidence agrees with it.

## Product character

Calm, legible, trustworthy, direct, and evidence-led. Human language comes
first; implementation detail is available on demand.

## Avoid

- Code-first IDE and agent-console conventions.
- Generic AI chat as the whole product.
- Decorative architecture diagrams or inventories without a user decision.
- Confidence claims without evidence or declared verification limits.

## Design principles

- Show what the application does, who acts, what changes, and what needs attention.
- Show relationships and journeys before internal identifiers.
- Keep intent, builder output, and verifier evidence visibly distinct.
- Offer one clear next action at a time.
- Use the existing Seed and System Model; never create a second product model.
- Let one person switch between Product, Frontend, Backend, Architecture, AI
  Behavior, and Verification lenses over that same core. Roles are
  responsibilities, not separate agents or teams.
- Show the verification plan before building: every approved item is labeled
  deterministic, runtime, advisory, recorded-only, or currently unsupported.
- Keep role advice advisory; only human ratification and deterministic
  reconciliation can change authority or readiness.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA basics: keyboard access, semantic landmarks, visible focus,
status text that does not rely on color, and reduced-motion support.
